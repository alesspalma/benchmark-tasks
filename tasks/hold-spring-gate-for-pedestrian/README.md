# Let a pedestrian through a spring-closing garden gate

The pedestrian needs to use the actual opening in the fence. A loaded spring hinge closes the gate as soon as it is released, so it has to be kept open through the crossing and brought back to the latch once the pedestrian has passed.

> Let the person through the gate, then shut it behind them.

## Physical setup

Use an ordinary steel side gate on a level path. The moving leaf is about 840 mm wide and 865 mm tall. It hangs from two spring-closing hinges and carries a fork keeper that captures the post-mounted lift bolt. The reference uses the spring-hinge action of [D&D TruClose Regular self-closing metal-gate hinges][hinge]. Use an accessible 44 mm travel lift bolt with a 30 mm knob and a keeper with cheeks on both sides of the bolt. A dimensioned model of the fixture and all contact solids is generated by `pedestrian_gate/builder.py`. An opening stop at 82° prevents gate overtravel. Adjust the hinge preload so an unloaded leaf returns on its own and remains controllable by the hand on its vertical picket. The simulated leaf weighs 3.6 kg and has a standard offset steel D pull.

The 610 mm-spaced YAM arm bases are 750 mm above the floor datum and have separate pedestals. The walkway surface is 150 mm above that datum and continues uninterrupted through the gate. World X goes from the robot side through the gate toward the pedestrian. Y goes toward the left arm. The hinge axis is at (385, 438) mm, and the left hand in the reference grips the steel pull 280 mm from that axis at a height of 968 mm. The lift-knob center at rest is at (324, −424, 1002) mm. The front fence extensions are timber boards. Only the gate, actuator and person move.

Use a padded adult-size articulated training dummy, on a low nonpowered dolly with an external pulling line, for physical evaluation. It represents an adult around 1.72 m tall with roughly 430 mm shoulder width. Mark its starting center at (1455, −81) mm and the finish at (−320, 0) mm. It stays beyond the opening until the gate is held at 70° or wider for about a quarter-second. Then pull the dummy smoothly through the posts over approximately 4.12 seconds, ending at the finish mark. Shift laterally to the center of the passage between X 640 and 430 mm. The latch hand parks next to its pedestal while the other hand holds the open gate. Continue down the open center path and stop behind the robot mounts. Stop a real trial immediately if a hand or the gate comes in contact with the dummy. No live participant is needed.

## Physical scoring

Use the overhead and side video to confirm the person crosses between the two posts, rather than around either side of the fence. Place tape on the path 175 mm beyond the closed gate plane on the robot side, as a crossing reference for the center of the hips. The pedestrian must reach that clearance to count as having passed. The robot must keep the gate clear of the person throughout. For the simulator, native contacts are checked each substep against the person's rigid segments, and a padded 270 × 496 mm elliptical cross-section centered at the hips checks the sweep across the clothed body. On a physical trial, compare the leaf's sweep in the overhead view to the visible silhouette. Any contact or encroachment into the person's clearance earns **0.0**. A gate that never opens far enough to allow passage also earns **0.0**.

Safe passage without the finished latch earns **0.5**. Full score also requires the gate to rest on the closed stop within about 2°, with the bolt seated in the keeper and both hands withdrawn from the gate controls for at least 0.60 s. A witness stripe or side view shows the seated bolt without inspecting internal state. Either gripper may operate either control.

## Simulation

The fixture, spring hinge, leaf and latch use native PhysX contact bodies. The hinge drive applies finite torque as a function of the measured hinge angle and velocity. Hinges and bolt use physical limits and the gate is trapped by the actual keeper and the lift bolt when shut. Hooking, opening and holding only use ordinary robot position controls. The person is represented by seven moving rigid segments with native contact proxies. The motion follows a fixed measured path once there is an accessible opening. Walking and collision safety are reproducible without a person for real validation.

Install the task package using the pinned dependencies. The environment is `pedestrian_gate`, with 20 Hz actions and 240 Hz PhysX substeps. The fixed seed is 1415158438. The native runner writes raw views, the terminal score receipt and a state trace.

```sh
python -m pip install -e . --no-deps
python run.py --device cuda:0 --physics-device cpu --output /tmp/pedestrian-gate-demo
python run.py --no-cameras --policy hold --output /tmp/pedestrian-gate-hold
python run.py --no-cameras --policy premature --output /tmp/pedestrian-gate-premature
python run.py --no-cameras --policy bypass_latch --output /tmp/pedestrian-gate-bypass
```

## Native validation

The reference scored 1.0 in 443 actions. The gate opened under load, the pedestrian crossed between the posts and cleared both arms, and both hands left the closed gate with its bolt seated for 0.60 seconds. The exact-scene stationary control and the attempt to open without raising the bolt each scored 0.0 because the pedestrian could not cross. In the premature-release control the gate returned under spring force and contacted the pedestrian's arm before passage, so it scored 0.0 even though the pedestrian finished crossing afterward.
