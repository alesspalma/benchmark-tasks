# Asset sources and licences

The boot, runner, two guard cups, spring indicators, rest and gear bag are original, generated geometry under the MIT licence in this folder. No vendor media, meshes or marks are included. The Jackson Ultima links in the README document the physical mechanism.

The robot and bench assets come from the installed [Inspect Robots](https://github.com/robocurve/inspect_robots) YAM/Arena runtime. Robot and rig assets retain their respective upstream licences and are loaded by the runtime.
