# Full-size reconstruction

Use standard 250 mL low-form beakers (70 mm outside diameter × 95 mm height),
a 30 × 8 mm PTFE stirring bar and a commercial external bar restrainer with
central stem/thumb grip. The task's engineering reconstruction of that tool
is provided in `restrainer.scad` in millimetres; it is not OEM CAD. Its exterior
matches the simulation's 80 × 25 × 10 mm face, 16 × 27 mm stem and 32 × 7 mm
thumb disk. Source/receiver mats are 100 × 100 × 4 mm; tool mat is
180 × 160 × 4 mm. The catch tray's inside footprint is 550 × 580 mm with a
4 mm base, 25 mm walls and 5 mm wall thickness.

For a custom restrainer, permanently encapsulate a magnet core in the split
case. The SCAD core is 12 mm along local X, 10 mm along Y and 8 mm along Z,
magnetized through its 8 mm thickness, normal to the tool face. The
corresponding dipole moment is an engineering estimate of 1.0 A m². The closed
pocket leaves at least 0.9 mm of case over either face. Tune the magnet against force–separation measurements,
not the exterior's blue colour or nominal grade alone. The 0.16 A m² stir-bar
moment must likewise be measured. Use ballast away from the core to obtain
70 g total tool mass. Do not add a robotic handle, latch or suction attachment.

The simulation geometry is generated by `magnetic_decant/builder.py`; the
beaker is an open hollow vessel, not a solid cylinder. The analytic water
material acts on the native vessels' mass, centre of mass and inertia.

An inexpensive reset uses 150 mL clean water, the same reusable bar and
vessels, nonslip rubber sheets and a plastic catch tray. Use a 1 g balance or
graduated cylinder to judge collected/spilled volume and ordinary cameras to
judge continuous bar retention, upright support and hands clear. Dry the
outside of the glass and gripper pads before restoring the initial layout.
