# Measure robot-joint lost motion under reversed proof loads

Robot actuator qualification measures how far the output turns between two small opposing torques while the reducer input is fixed. This is a material setting for position reversals because elastic lost motion remains even when gearing has little free backlash. The fixture uses the paired, approximately ±4% rated torque method in the engineering reference. [Harmonic Drive engineering reference](https://harmonicdrive.de/fileadmin/Downloads/Produkte/Kataloge/Harmonic_Drive_Gears_EN_1053522.pdf) · [Manual robot-joint measurement method](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10845-022-01934-z)

## Physical fixture

Use a de-energized low-torque reducer on a rigid bench in the YAM station. The reducer and spring fixture are a compact bench calibration unit. The target loads in this task suit a nominal 10 N·m output. No powered robot axis is necessary.

- The fixed steel plate is 258 × 335 × 9 mm. Its center is 422 mm forward and 20 mm left of the midpoint of the two arm bases. Bench height is 747.5 mm. The output turns about the vertical axis at an axle height of 850 mm. The round face is 112 mm across.
- Fit a stiff steel lever to the output flange. Put the center of the blue 41 × 26 × 36 mm bidirectional grip 115 mm from the output axis toward the right arm, at 890 mm height. Its carriage slides tangentially against opposed, centered springs with combined stiffness 300 N/m. The test marks indicate ±11.6 mm of carriage travel relative to the flange, for ±0.40 N·m. Check the force scale with a spring force gauge before a run. The white center and proof marks allow a human to judge direction and free return.
- Fit a spring-returned guided lock yoke to the frame on the input side. The gold 53 × 49 mm pad starts centered 142 mm left of the arm midpoint, at 901 mm height. Its 14 mm downstroke brings the steel yoke fingers around the opposing sides of the reducer input's 32 × 39 mm reaction key. The pad must be manually held in the lower band during both force readings. Use rigid steel and a return compression spring. The jaw and blade coordinates in `builder.py` specify the lock clearances.
- Place an angle readout facing the operator. Use a bench encoder or a digital indicator contacting the output tangentially at a measured radius. Convert tangential displacement s to an angle of arcsin(s/r), where r is the indicator radius. At a radius of 115 mm, 1 arcmin is about 0.033 mm of tangential displacement. Zero the gauge unloaded before applying either force. Display or write the negative and positive readings and their difference in arcminutes. Use a second indicator on the input reaction key to check that it stays within the specified angular tolerance. The native fixture meshes are generated by `joint_lost_motion/builder.py`.
- The two output readings must be consistent with the corresponding load direction. Measure relative shaft deflection using the output reading minus the input reading, with at least 2 arcmin after the load reverses. The final lost motion is the difference between the two output readings.

The two arms start open in their standard ready positions. The lock and carriage start freely returned, both readings blank. To reset, remove the load, release the input lock, clear the reading registers, and zero the scale. The same fixed assembly is used for the reference and hosted runs.

## Scoring on physical hardware

Use fixed video, the handle witness marks and the readout. The yoke must be depressed at least 9 mm and the input must remain within 16 arcmin of zero. Register each signed torque reading separately after at least 0.55 seconds within 0.32–0.48 N·m magnitude and at most 3 arcmin of output-angle variation. Record the median output angle during that settled interval. Either signed load can come first. Either hand can take either role whenever the physical configuration allows it.

| Score | Physical outcome |
| --- | --- |
| 0.00 | No valid reading, or the two readings do not give an 8–70 arcmin span on the specified calibration unit. |
| 0.40 | One direction has a settled reading. |
| 0.80 | Both directions have settled readings with an 8–70 arcmin span. |
| 1.00 | The two readings and span are recorded. For 0.45 s, the handle is below 0.07 N·m, the lock is within 3 mm of its raised stop and both grippers are at least 55 mm clear. |

Use a sample reducer whose independently checked span falls within the test window before collection. The readout can be checked with a second external dial and a known moment arm. The simulation uses native rigid contact and native linear and torsional springs. The compact fixture applies the measurement principle without modeling gear teeth, the full hysteresis loop or electronic encoder noise.

## Run

Use Python 3.12 and the repository's Isaac Lab–Arena setup. The Inspect Robots embodiment is `joint_lost_motion`. The fixed seed is 1415158438. Each trial allows up to 540 actions at 20 Hz, or 27 seconds of simulated time. The scripted reference sends ordinary position and gripper commands through the same station interface.

```sh
python -m pip install -e . --no-deps
python run.py --device cuda:0 --physics-device cpu --output /tmp/joint-demo
python run.py --no-cameras --policy hold --output /tmp/joint-hold
python run.py --no-cameras --policy one_way --output /tmp/joint-one-way
python run.py --no-cameras --policy unlocked --output /tmp/joint-unlocked
```

The runner prints and saves the physical measurements and score. Camera runs save the initial frame and each action frame from all three native cameras. Geometry and task code use the included MIT license. The calibrated arms and camera models come from the pinned [YAM Arena dependency](https://github.com/kairosiann/yam-isaac-lab-arena/tree/0059d02a1a91bc6834c1d0846022891df0c78730).
