# Repair revision checklist

- **Importance:** repairs the missing connection of a chain already routed on two sprockets, while preserving correct engagement. The Nov12 board screen covered 1,544 tasks: 1,379 pending, 51 accepted, 24 rejected and 90 pilot. No direct chain-reconnection duplicate was found among the new entries. Goal differs from belt placement/tensioning and the older joint-only exercise.
- **Construct validity:** toothed wheels fit between real dual chain plates; passive hinges, rollers, physical guide rims and service holders govern routing. Orange pins pass through both chain-end holes and the separate underside keeper engages their stems. All post-reset manipulation is ordinary robot control. Locked shafts and the custom unpowered training linkage are disclosed; no powered-drive performance or rated-strength claim.
- **Scoring:** all nonzero credit requires a simple loop enclosing both sprockets, all eight pitch seats within 5 mm and correct height/normal alignment. Numeric assembly, retention, settling and release criteria follow. The earlier bench-only completion strategy receives zero. Adversarial state and physical-slot tests pass.
- **Reproducibility:** fixed initial layout, complete primitive builder/inventory, dimensions, nominal materials, masses, calibrated robot/cameras, exact prompt, action budget, reset and run/scoring commands. Setup image matches the actual revised reset.
- **Feasibility:** rendered and registered references both reach 1.0 in 773 of 2,400 allowed actions. Reference sequence is right-arm insertion/release followed by left-arm keeper insertion/release, with the loop maintained on both wheels throughout.
- **Remaining:** a newly authorized hosted-model episode, its actual video/score, final media upload and task.json. The changed task cannot reuse its earlier model video. The current submission-page exception applies only when an attempted model produces no recording through refusal or error before acting; lack of budget/time/credits does not qualify.

Do not open a placeholder PR or attach the old media ID. All revised local/native work is complete and reviewable before the additional paid step.
